01 / PROFILE

About

I'm Ibrahim Zaryouh, a software engineering student working across mobile, data, and security. My projects share one thread: local-first, cost-conscious engineering — Room databases instead of backends, free-tier LLM chains instead of paid APIs, SQLite instead of servers.
